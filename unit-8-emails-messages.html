Warm-Up

Read the Room

A message changes with the person receiving it.

What is the difference between a message to a close friend and one to a teacher?

Go further: Which small word makes the biggest difference?

Possible answer
I say 'Hi' to a friend and 'Dear Sensei' to my teacher. The word 'please' changes more than I expected.

When did a short message make you feel suddenly awkward?

Go further: What would you rewrite now?

Possible answer
I sent 'We need to talk' as a joke. I would add a silly emoji next time so nobody worries.
Grammar in Context

Openers and Closers

Informal: Hi Yuki / See you. Formal: Dear Ms Sato / Kind regards.

Write one line asking a teacher for extra time. Keep it polite and direct.

Go further: Which word makes the request softer?

Possible answer
Could I have one more day to finish the essay, please? 'Could' makes it soft.

Turn 'Send me the notes' into a message to a classmate you do not know well.

Go further: What changed besides the words?

Possible answer
Could you send me the notes from today when you have a moment? The tone feels less demanding.
Message Practice

Reply With Care

A good reply answers the question and adds one useful thing.

A friend writes: I am nervous about the presentation. What do you write back?

Go further: What would you avoid saying?

Possible answer
I would say I am nervous too and ask which part worries them. I would avoid saying not to worry.

A teacher writes: Please bring your notebook tomorrow. How do you reply?

Go further: Why is a short reply enough here?

Possible answer
Thank you, I will bring it. The teacher needs confirmation, not a story.

A classmate sends a message at midnight asking for homework answers. What do you do?

Go further: How do you say no without ending the friendship?

Possible answer
I say I can explain the first question in the morning. I do not send the answers.

Writing Practice

Two Messages

Write both. Say them aloud before you send anything.

Ask a friend to help you practise for a test. Keep it friendly and specific.

Go further: What detail makes your request easy to say yes to?

Possible answer
Can we do ten minutes of speaking practice before lunch? I will bring the questions.

Apologise to a teacher for handing in work late. Give a reason without making excuses.

Go further: Which words keep the tone honest?

Possible answer
I am sorry the essay is late. I underestimated the time I needed and I have finished it now.
Roleplay

Inbox Mystery

You receive a message with one missing detail. Ask for it politely.

The message says: Meet me after school. Your turn: ask where.

Go further: How do you sound friendly without sounding demanding?

Possible answer
Sure, where should we meet? I will be there after club.

The message says: I sent the file. You cannot find it. What do you write?

Go further: How do you avoid blaming them?

Possible answer
Thanks, I cannot see it yet. Could you send it again?

Challenge

Say It Three Ways

One idea, three tones: friend, classmate, teacher.

Ask for the homework in all three tones.

Go further: Which tone felt hardest, and why?

Possible answer
Friend: send me the homework. Classmate: did you catch the homework? Teacher: could you repeat the homework, please? The teacher tone felt hardest.

Which tone do you use most often online?

Go further: Has it ever caused a misunderstanding?

Possible answer
The short friend tone. Once a classmate thought I was angry because I wrote only 'fine'.
Go Deeper

What Messages Do

Messages are small, but they carry a lot of weight.

Why do people write things in messages they would never say out loud?

Go further: When does that help, and when does it hurt?

Possible answer
Typing gives you time to think. It helps when you are shy, but it hurts when people forget there is a person reading.

What do you check before pressing send?

Go further: Who taught you to check?

Possible answer
I check the name at the top. My sister taught me after she sent a complaint to the wrong teacher.

Which message from this year would you keep forever?

Go further: Why that one?

Possible answer
A thank-you from a friend after a bad day. It is short, but I still know exactly where I was when I read it.

Who will you write to first?
