Mid-Year Review

Describing, present perfect continuous, reported speech, passive voice. All in one conversation.

Press → or Space to begin. The palette icon changes colours.

Warm-Up

How Far Have You Come?

Look back four months. Talk about change, not perfection.

What can you say in English now that you could not say at the start of the year?

Go further: Which moment made you notice the change?

Possible answer
I can tell a story with sequence words now. I noticed when a new student asked me the way and I did not freeze.

Which mistake from this year taught you the most?

Go further: What do you do differently because of it?

Possible answer
I used to say 'I am studying English since three years'. Now I catch myself before the sentence ends.
Four Patterns

Mix the Grammar

Choose the pattern that fits your idea. Do not force all four into one answer.

Describe your school using one passive sentence and one adjective.

Go further: Why does the passive feel natural here?

Possible answer
The corridors are painted pale green. I do not know who painted them, so passive works better.

Use present perfect continuous to talk about something you started last term.

Go further: What does that tense add that the simple form does not?

Possible answer
I have been keeping a word notebook since April. It shows the habit is still going.
Review Round

Four Corners

Each corner has a different grammar focus. Answer three of them.

Describe a corner of your classroom in vivid detail.

Go further: Which adjective carries the most information?

Possible answer
The back corner is shabby and quiet, with a stack of old exercise books. 'Shabby' does most of the work.

What have you been doing after school recently?

Go further: What made you start?

Possible answer
I have been walking home past the river instead of taking the bus. A friend suggested it once and I kept it.

Report one surprising thing a classmate said this month.

Go further: How did you keep it kind?

Possible answer
She said she had never seen the sea. I told my friend she was surprised by that, and I did not say it as a joke.

What object in your bag was made in another country?

Go further: How do you know?

Possible answer
My pencil was made in China. The label is small but readable.

Speaking Practice

Two-Minute Catch-Up

You have two minutes. Talk about one change, one habit, and one thing someone said.

Which change in your English are you proudest of?

Go further: What did you do to make it happen?

Possible answer
I speak without translating every word now. I started reading short English stories on the train.

Who helped you this term without being asked?

Go further: What would you like to say back to them?

Possible answer
My deskmate checks my sentences when I ask. I would like to tell her she made me less nervous.
Picture Review

A Term in Three Pictures

Choose three images from the term. Tell the story between them.

Which picture would show the start of the term?

Go further: What is missing from that picture now?

Possible answer
An empty notebook on the first desk. What is missing is the fear of speaking.

Which picture would show a turning point?

Go further: What happened in the minute after it?

Possible answer
Me standing at the front of the class. After it I sat down and my hands were still shaking, but I had finished.

Challenge

Teach It Back

Pick one pattern from this year and explain it to a partner using your own example.

Which grammar point is easiest for you to teach?

Go further: What example makes it clear?

Possible answer
Present perfect continuous. I use the word notebook example because you can see the habit.

Which point still feels awkward?

Go further: What question would you ask your teacher about it?

Possible answer
Reported speech with pronouns. I would ask how to keep 'my' and 'your' straight.
Go Deeper

The Part Nobody Grades

Talk about the habits and people behind the grammar.

What did you learn about yourself as a learner this term?

Go further: How will you use that next term?

Possible answer
I learn better when I say a sentence aloud before I write it. I will keep doing that.

Which small habit helped you most?

Go further: Where did it come from?

Possible answer
Writing three new words on my hand before class. I copied it from a friend and never stopped.

If you could thank one person from this term, what would you say?

Go further: What would they say back?

Possible answer
I would thank my teacher for not correcting every sentence at once. She would probably say I did the work myself.
Wrap-Up

Carry It Forward

Write one sentence in each pattern. Keep them in your notebook for next term.

Which of the four patterns will you use outside class first?

Go further: Where exactly?

Possible answer
Present perfect continuous. I will use it when someone asks what I have been doing after school.
